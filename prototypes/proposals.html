What changes

  • Bar is centred, sized to its content, and floats space-3 below the top edge on a 1px border-subtle ring.
  • Sticky, with shadow-xs at rest and shadow-md once the page scrolls.
  • No current-page indicator: on a one-page site every link is an anchor, so there’s no “current page” to mark. Hover is a color-bg-surface wash.
  • Links fit inline on phones (the name hides), so there’s no menu button on the landing page.

Why

  • Matches itti’s quiet centred nav while keeping your brand mark and theme toggle.
  • Floating keeps the header present without a full-width band competing with the hero.
  • The current full-width header stays for case-study pages, where “Back to work” matters more.

Accessibility and tokens

  • Keyboard focus stays visible (WCAG 2.4.7 requires it). I removed the selected state, not the focus ring. The ring is drawn inside each item.
  • Items are 32px with a pointer and 44px on touch.
  • Tokens: color-bg-elevated, color-border-subtle, shadow-xs/md, radius-md bar, radius-sm items. Radius stays md, not a pill, to keep the shape rule.

What changes

  • Centred stack: one sentence at type-h3 (24px). Your name is in primary text, the rest in secondary.
  • “Currently designing →” in mono, plus a chip with a live dot (the itti pattern, in system shapes).
  • Two quiet actions: Copy email (copies, then shows “Copied”) and Résumé. The big blue button goes; the work grid becomes the call to action.

Why

  • A smaller headline reads as confident rather than loud, and the cards below become the first visual.
  • Copy email avoids mailto:, which often fails for people without a mail app set up.
  • The spec-sheet panel moves down into About, where it supports the bio.

Accessibility and tokens

  • One h1 per page (it’s an h2 here only because this review page has its own h1).
  • The copy result is announced through a polite live region. The dot pulses only without reduced motion.
  • Tokens: type-h3, text-primary/secondary, font-mono, state-success, shadow-xs, space-6/16/20.

What changes

  • No outline. The artwork sits on a neutral stage (color-bg-surface), rising from the bottom edge like a screen on a desk.
  • Hover works on the whole card: the stage deepens to color-bg-sunken, the artwork lifts 8px, and a ↗ chip appears. The title no longer underlines.
  • Text is simplified to title + mono year on one line, then a single “Discipline · Area” line. Summary and tags move into the case study.
  • Staggered two-column grid, with the right column offset 96px (the itti rhythm).

Why

  • The stage makes every project read as a curated artifact, even when screenshots vary in colour.
  • Hover on the image is the clearest signal that the whole card is clickable.
  • Less text per card makes the grid scannable.

Accessibility and tokens

  • The whole card remains one link (one tab stop). Focus shows the ring around the card and reveals the ↗ chip.
  • The lift turns off with reduced motion; the tone change remains.
  • Tokens: color-bg-surface/sunken, shadow-md on the artwork, radius-md stage, radius-sm screen, space-8/12 insets.
